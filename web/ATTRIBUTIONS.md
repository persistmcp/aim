## Exercise illustrations

The illustrations under `web/public/exercises/` are third party works licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike. They are **not** covered by this project's MIT
licence and stay under CC BY-SA.

We host our own copies: `backend/scripts/import_pool_images.py` downloads each source
image, trims, resizes and converts it to webp under `web/public/exercises/<slug>-N.webp`.
Nothing is hotlinked. Processing does not change the terms, so those derived copies remain
CC BY-SA under the same version as their source, and the credit has to travel with them —
the app renders it as a caption next to every illustration it shows
(`web/src/app/components/ProgramSection.tsx`).

Two frames ship per movement, a start and an end position.
